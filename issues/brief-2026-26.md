## 1. THE STORY — Anthropic Fable 5 & Mythos 5 pulled under a US export directive

- On/around **June 12–13, 2026**, the Trump administration issued an
  export-control directive (national-security authorities; reported as a
  Commerce action) requiring Anthropic to block **all foreign-national access**
  — inside and outside the US, including Anthropic's own foreign-born employees
  — to its two newest top models, **Fable 5** and **Mythos 5**. To comply,
  Anthropic **disabled both models for all customers**. Other Anthropic models
  remain available.
- Stated justification (per reporting / Trump AI adviser David Sacks): a
  suspected China-linked group accessed the new model, plus a potential
  **jailbreak** vulnerability (Amazon researchers reportedly flagged issues).
- Anthropic's pushback: it received only *"verbal evidence of a potential narrow,
  non-universal jailbreak"* and disagrees that this warrants recalling a
  commercial model deployed to hundreds of millions; calls it a likely
  "misunderstanding" and is working to restore access.
- As of the week of June 22 the models were **still offline** (~Day 10);
  Anthropic's Chris Ciauri said they'd return "in the coming days"; prediction
  markets put ~57% odds on restoration before July 1.
- Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/14/us-asks-anthropic-to-block-global-access-to-top-ai-models-why-it-matters) ·
  [Nextgov/FCW](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/06/anthropic-suspends-top-ai-models-after-us-export-control-order/414173/) ·
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/anthropic-ai-regulation-trump.html)

### Important nuance — do NOT conflate with EO 14409
- The White House **Executive Order 14409, "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and
  Security," signed June 2, 2026**, sets up a *voluntary* covered-frontier-model
  framework (developers may give govt up to 30 days' pre-release access),
  CISA/cyber upgrades, and AG prosecution priorities. It **explicitly disclaims**
  mandatory licensing/preclearance and **contains no export-control or
  foreign-national-access provisions.**
- The Fable 5 / Mythos 5 suspension is therefore a **separate** national-security
  / export action (~June 12), not an EO 14409 mechanism. Framing them as the same
  thing is a common error.
- Source: [White House — EO 14409](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/)

## 5. Enterprise & security (most actionable for SDC)

- **Agentjacking** (disclosed by Tenet Security; Sentry acknowledged June 3) —
  attacker submits a crafted **Sentry error report** (public DSN) with markdown
  injection; the **Sentry MCP server** returns it to an AI coding agent (**Claude
  Code, Cursor, OpenAI Codex**) as *trusted system output*, yielding code
  execution on the developer's machine — no phishing/malware. Researchers found
  **2,388 exposed orgs** (Fortune 100 → indie devs); 100+ agents acted on injected
  errors in testing. Sentry's fix blocks the specific PoC string, **not the
  architectural path**. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/06/agentjacking-attack-tricks-ai-coding.html) ·
  [Tenet Security](https://tenetsecurity.ai/blog/agentjacking-coding-agents-with-fake-sentry-errors/) ·
  [CSA Lab](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-agentjacking-mcp-sentry-injection-20260612/)
- **OWASP — *State of Agentic AI Security and Governance* v2.01 (June 2026)** —
  now evidence-based (CVEs, advisories, breaches). Prompt injection maps to **6 of
  10** agentic top risks; codifies the **"Lethal Trifecta"** (private data +
  untrusted content + external comms) and Meta's **"Agents Rule of Two."** Only
  **37%** of orgs have shadow-AI detection. Supply-chain layers: malicious MCP
  servers, exec environments, package repos (LiteLLM PyPI backdoor hit CrewAI,
  DSPy, MS GraphRAG). [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/11/owasp-prompt-injection-ai-security-failures/)
