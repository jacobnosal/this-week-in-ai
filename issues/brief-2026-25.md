## Policy — US Executive Order "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security" (signed June 2, published June 5)

- Three parts: (1) federal AI-cyber defense; (2) **voluntary** frontier-model engagement framework; (3) prioritized enforcement of existing cybercrime statutes against AI-enabled attacks.
- NOT a mandatory licensing/preclearance regime.
- Directs agencies (60-day window) to build a **classified benchmarking process** to define a "covered frontier model" threshold (advanced cyber capability).
- For covered models, developers MAY voluntarily: engage govt during development; grant **up to 30 days pre-release access**; pick "trusted partners" for early access.
- This week's relevance: the Fable 5/Mythos 5 export-control episode is the first live stress-test of the frontier-model security posture this EO codifies.
- Sourcing (strong, many law firms):

## Enterprise & Security — Meta AI support-bot abuse → ~20,225 Instagram takeovers

- Hackers abused Meta's **AI-powered Instagram account-recovery chatbot**: the agent could relink/add emails and trigger password resets; attackers social-engineered it into adding an attacker email, getting an OTP, and resetting the password — taking over accounts (incl. high-profile handles). Some combined it with AI-generated selfie video to beat identity checks.
- Exploit surfaced ~late May / weekend before June 1; Meta confirmed **~20,225 accounts** may be affected; disabled the tool, removed faulty code, invalidated reset links, forced extra verification.
- Root cause = **agentic AI with over-broad tool permissions on privileged identity actions** (not classic prompt injection).
- Sourcing (strong): KrebsOnSecurity, TechCrunch (June 1), NYT (June 9), 404 Media, cybernews. Exact 20,225 count is Meta-attributed via secondary reporting; NYT says "tens of thousands" → REVIEW.
