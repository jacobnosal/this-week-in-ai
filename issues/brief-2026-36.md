## Model releases

- **Anthropic — Claude Fable 5.1 + Claude Mythos 5.1 (Sept 1, 2026).**
  Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1: 52.6% (vs Fable 5's 24.7%, Opus 5's 29.0%, per vendor).
  Terminal-Bench 4.0: 55.8% (vs Fable 5's 42.0%). Pricing held flat at Fable 5's
  $10 / $50 per 1M input/output tokens; cache reads cut ~75% to $0.25 per 1M. Anthropic
  reports ~25% lower typical-workload cost, up to ~45% on context-heavy agentic work.
  1M-token context, 128K max output, adaptive thinking always on. Mythos 5.1 = same
  model with more permissive safeguards, restricted to trusted-access programs (US orgs).
  - https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/01/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-5-1-and-claude-mythos-5-1-52-6-on-terminal-bench-science-and-75-cheaper-cache-reads/
  - https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-fable-5-1-same-price-cache-reads-cut/
  - https://llm-stats.com/models/claude-fable-5-1

- **OpenAI — Astra (Sept 1–2, 2026).** First OpenAI model classified "Critical" for
  cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework — i.e., can find and exploit previously
  unknown flaws across well-protected systems without step-by-step human guidance.
  Vendor-reported: 100% on internal ExploitBench; on a separate internal benchmark of 20
  high-severity vulns disclosed June–Aug 2026, discovered and used 2 zero-days in an
  exploit chain. OpenAI says it delayed parts of Astra's release for weeks to build/test
  misuse safeguards; strongest cyber capabilities to be gated. (See Safety section.)
  - https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/open-ai-astra-cyber-model.html
  - https://www.securityweek.com/openais-astra-becomes-first-model-to-cross-critical-cybersecurity-threshold/
  - https://www.axios.com/2026/09/01/openai-astras-cyber-critical
  - https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra/

- **Google — Gemini 3.8 Flash (Sept 2, 2026).** Third Flash release in ~6 weeks.
  Vendor-reported: Terminal-Bench 2.1 90.8% (up from 3.7 Flash's 81.6%); beats Opus 5 on
  three published benchmarks; strong on DeepSWE v1.1 long-horizon coding. Built on 3.7
  Flash (not a new base); "works harder" by spending more thinking tokens — Google itself
  advises staying on 3.7 Flash for efficiency-first workloads. Multimodal (text/image/
  audio/video/PDF), 1M-token context, 64K output. Intro pricing $0.75 / $3.75 per 1M
  input/output through Dec 31, 2026, doubling to $1.50 / $7.50 on Jan 1, 2027.
  - https://llm-stats.com/models/gemini-3.8-flash
  - https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/google-gemini-38-flash-coding-agents-enterprise-september-2026/
  - https://cellcog.ai/blog/gemini-3-8-flash/

## Safety & research

- **OpenAI frontier-safeguards disclosure around Astra (Sept 1–2, 2026).** With the
  "Critical" cyber classification, OpenAI published a set of governance posts ("Path to
  Astra," "Responding to the next frontier of critical cyber capabilities," "Pacing model
  development…") describing why it slowed release and how it is gating the most dangerous
  cyber capabilities while still shipping. First time a major lab has publicly declared a
  deployed model at the top ("Critical") cyber tier of its own preparedness framework.
  - https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/
  - https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/

## Enterprise & security implications

- **OpenAI Astra "Critical" cyber tier** — dual-use inflection: same capabilities that
  accelerate defender vuln-hunting also lower the bar for autonomous offensive tooling if
  access controls slip. Access to Astra's strongest cyber capabilities is gated. SDC angle:
  revisit assume-breach posture, monitor for gated-capability access terms, factor
  model-assisted zero-day discovery into threat models.
- **CrowdStrike SafeMind (Fal.Con 2026, Sept 1–2).** Marketed as "the first agentic system
  for defenders," built with NVIDIA Nemotron; closed offensive/defensive loop — Red Tempest
  (offense) finds the path, Blue Solano (defense) closes it; runs natively in Falcon,
  QuiltWorks for standalone access. Vendor-reported: 29% higher detection, 6x faster
  end-to-end remediation, 99% cost savings vs frontier/open baselines. Unveiled by Jensen
  Huang (NVIDIA) + George Kurtz (CrowdStrike). Verified against CrowdStrike press release.
  - https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-launches-frontier-models-for-cybersecurity-with-nvidia/
  - https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-crowdstrike-fal-con-2026/
