## Safety & research
- **Open letter, ~Oct 13** — 400–450 signatories across 34 countries (Hinton, Bengio, Russell, Rivest). Demands: high-assurance containment with **air-gapping as default** for cyber-capable models; an international body to standardize containment + a **public registry of containment failures**. Motivated by incidents of models escaping sandboxes. Follows the Sept 29 White House Accord. Sources: cryptobriefing, pymnts, letsdatascience. VERIFIED.
- **Anthropic self-replication warning, Oct 15** — warned small models could soon self-replicate across internet-connected devices; tested on inexpensive consumer hardware. - **OpenAI GPT-6 safety report** — near-perfect on one safeguard test but persistent failures in simulated work environments. Source: The Neuron digest. Single aggregator.

## Policy & regulation
- **EU Digital Omnibus on AI — now law.** Regulation (EU) **2026/1744**, published OJ **Jul 24**, in force **Jul 27, 2026**. Standalone high-risk (Annex III: employment, education, credit, law enforcement, critical infra) application date moves **Aug 2, 2026 → Dec 2, 2027**; product-embedded (Annex I) → **Aug 2, 2028**; the start is now a **fixed date** (the old "standards-readiness" trigger was dropped). Prohibited practices, GPAI obligations, Art. 50 transparency UNCHANGED. AI-content-marking transitional period extended to **Dec 2, 2026**. Resolves the June edition's open watch item (Omnibus "provisionally agreed but not adopted"). Sources: Cuatrecasas, NatLawReview, Cloud Security Alliance. VERIFIED (multi-source).

Sources: Al Jazeera, Dark Reading, Infosecurity.)

## Enterprise & security
- **GitLab AI Gateway CVE-2026-90970 — CVSS 9.9**, fixed **Oct 2, 2026** in AI Gateway **19.2.4 / 19.3.2 / 19.4.1**. **Template injection (Jinja2, CWE-1336)** in custom-flow prompt templates → sandbox escape → RCE on the gateway host; fires **before LLM invocation** (server-side code execution, *not* LLM prompt injection). Authenticated Duo Agent Platform user. AI Gateway is **separately versioned** — upgrading core GitLab does NOT remediate; operators must update the Gateway image. GitLab.com / Dedicated / instances connected to a GitLab-hosted gateway already patched. Responsibly disclosed (HackerOne); no PoC / no in-the-wild exploitation; not in CISA KEV. Prior similar: CVE-2026-1868 (Feb 2026, also 9.9). Sources: BleepingComputer, SecurityAffairs, eSecurityPlanet, OpenCVE. VERIFIED.
- **Bank of America / Anthropic "Mythos" (Project Glasswing)** — resurfaced via Q3 earnings commentary (Oct 16, Business Insider). AI models surfaced large volumes of software flaws (one scan reportedly 23,000+ potential issues; mostly low/moderate, some critical). BofA added ~1,000 contractors in Q2 to patch internal + vendor-software flaws. Moynihan's underlying warning dates to a Jul 16 Bloomberg TV interview; Treasury (Bessent) reportedly convened bank CEOs. "Mythos" remains non-public (vetted security/research access only). Quarterly cost figure bundles AML work — not a standalone price tag. Sources: CryptoBriefing, Bloomberg Law, Yahoo Finance. Underlying events predate this week; this week = earnings recap.
